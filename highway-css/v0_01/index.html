What you get

One file. Semantic tags pick up the road chrome on their own. Signs, route shields, meters, tags, and notes are classes. Point --hw-font, --hw-display, and --hw-mono at any faces. Omit the webfonts and the stacks fall back to system faces.

v0.01 is the first alpha. Dark and light ship together; the palette is the configuration surface.

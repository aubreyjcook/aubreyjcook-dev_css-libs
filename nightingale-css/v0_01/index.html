What you get

One file. Semantic tags pick up the night look on their own. Cards, tags, notes, meters, and the moon are classes. Point --ng-font, --ng-display, and --ng-script at any faces. Omit the webfonts and the stacks fall back to system faces.

v0.01 is the first alpha. Dark and light ship together; the palette is the configuration surface.

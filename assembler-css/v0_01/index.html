What you get

One file. Buttons, fields, tables, and type pick up the chrome on their own. Panels, meters, tags, and notes are classes. Point --asm-font and --asm-display at any face. Omit the webfonts and the stack falls back to the system mono.

v0.01 is the first alpha. Color is the configuration surface. Light and dark ship together.
